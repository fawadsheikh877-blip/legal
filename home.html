What we share

Hearts in Sujood is a small Islamic reminder page. Every day we share one short, painted, story-style video that ends with a verse from the Quran and its real recitation. Our tone is always positive: Allah's love, mercy and nearness.

📖

Quran verses

Each reel is built around one short passage, shown in Arabic and English, with the recitation of a well-known reciter.

🤲

Duas & reflections

Simple, warm reflections for hard days — patience, gratitude, hope and trust in Allah.

🌙

For children

Picture-book reels for kids aged 5–10: being thankful, being kind, and knowing that Allah made everything.

🎨

Calm, modest visuals

Soft watercolour scenes with modestly dressed, faceless characters — never images of prophets or angels.

How every reminder is checked

We take the words of Allah seriously. Before anything is posted, it goes through these steps:

Verified text

The verse is taken from two independent Quran databases and must match exactly.

Real recitation

Only recordings of human reciters are used — never AI-generated Quran recitation.

Careful review

The words and pictures are checked for accuracy, modesty and a gentle, positive tone.

Human approval

A member of our team watches every video and approves it before it is published on our own accounts.

Questions

Who makes these videos?

Hearts in Sujood is run by a small team. We use a private publishing tool on our own computer to prepare the videos, and every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is posted to our own Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

Do you use music?

Some reels have very soft background sound under the narration only. It always fades out completely before the Quran recitation begins.

Do you collect any data?

No. We don't collect information about our viewers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
