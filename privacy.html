Privacy Policy – Hearts in Sujood

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Hearts in Sujood ("we", "us") publishes short Islamic reminder videos on our own social media accounts. This policy explains how our publishing tool handles information.

1. Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the Hearts in Sujood team to publish our own videos to our own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information we access

When we connect our TikTok, Facebook or Instagram account, the platform gives us:

  • basic profile information of OUR account (such as display name and account ID), and
  • an access token that allows us to publish videos to that account.

We do not access, collect or store information about viewers, followers or any other users.

3. How the information is used

This information is used only to upload and publish our own videos to our own accounts after a team member manually approves each video.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are never shared, sold or given to anyone else.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

6. Removing access

We can disconnect the tool at any time from the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). On disconnection, the stored tokens are deleted.

7. Children

Our content is family friendly. The tool does not collect any information from children or any other users.
